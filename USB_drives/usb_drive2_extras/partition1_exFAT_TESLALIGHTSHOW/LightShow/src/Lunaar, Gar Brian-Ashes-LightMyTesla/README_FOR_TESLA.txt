Tesla Light Show USB Guide

1) Copy the entire "LightShow" folder from this ZIP to the ROOT of your USB drive.
2) Plug the USB drive into your Tesla.
3) Open Toybox -> Light Show (menu location may vary by model/software).

Your USB drive should look like this:

USB_ROOT/
  LightShow/
    <your-song>.mp3
    <your-song>.fseq

Tips:
- Do not nest the LightShow folder inside another folder.
- Keep file names unchanged.
